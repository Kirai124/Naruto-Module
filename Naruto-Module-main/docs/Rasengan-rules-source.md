## Gaining the Rasengan

The Rasengan is not merely learned — it is created through an understanding of chakra that very few shinobi ever achieve. It requires absolute control over chakra rotation, compression, and shape.

For Kirai, however, the Rasengan is only the beginning. His understanding of **Planetary Chakra** allows him to push the technique beyond its normal limits and develop his own variations of the Rasengan.

The path to mastering this technique begins with understanding the principles that make it possible.

##### Step 1: Understanding Rotation

You must first understand the three fundamental principles behind the Rasengan: **Rotation, Compression, and Containment.**

This requires 1 downtime spent studying advanced chakra-control principles and the theory behind the Rasengan, followed by a DC 20 Intelligence (Ninshou) check.

If you fail, your understanding remains incomplete. You must wait an additional week of downtime before attempting the check again.


##### Step 2: Creating the Core

Knowledge alone is not enough. You must prove that your body is capable of creating and controlling the chakra necessary to form a Rasengan.

During actual gameplay or a significant narrative event, you must create and maintain a stable sphere of rotating chakra under extreme circumstances.

The exact circumstances are determined by the DM, but the event must represent a meaningful breakthrough in your chakra control.

Examples include:

- Successfully controlling your chakra while severely injured or exhausted.
- Maintaining precise chakra control while protecting another creature.
- Discovering a new method of controlling chakra through your own experimentation.
- Successfully forming the core of the Rasengan during a moment where failure would have serious consequences.

The important part is not the specific challenge.

**The Rasengan must be created through your own understanding and skill.**


##### Step 3: The First Rasengan

Once the first two steps have been completed, you must spend 3 downtime perfecting the technique.

Each Downtime requires 250 Ryo for training materials, specialized equipment, seals, or other resources necessary for developing your chakra control.

At the end of the three Downtimes, you must succeed on a DC 18 Wisdom Ability check.

On a success, you finally compress your chakra into a stable rotating sphere.

**You have created the Rasengan.**

If you fail, your chakra destabilizes before the technique can be completed. You must go through the three Downtimes again before attempting the check.

___

Once complete, your chakra control has reached a level previously thought impossible, and you unlock **1st level in the Rasengan Class Mod**.

### Leveling Up the Rasengan

Progression in the Rasengan Class Mod does not come from simply repeating the same training exercises. The Rasengan evolves alongside its user, and every new level represents a deeper understanding of chakra manipulation and Planetary Chakra.

To gain another level in this Class Mod, you must demonstrate that your current understanding of the Rasengan is no longer sufficient.

##### Path of the Rasengan

Each time you wish to gain a new level in this Class Mod, you must accomplish a significant feat in which your Rasengan or understanding of Planetary Chakra plays a decisive role.

This may be defeating a powerful enemy, surviving an extreme situation, or discovering a new application of the Rasengan.

The exact requirements are determined by the DM, but the feat must demonstrate genuine growth in your mastery of the technique.

A simple victory is not enough.

**You must evolve.**

##### Transcendence Through Combat

Upon completing a Path of the Rasengan, you must spend a Long Rest reflecting on the experience and the way your chakra behaved throughout it.

During this rest, you analyze the rotation, compression, and flow of your chakra, searching for the next step in your understanding.

After the Long Rest, your chakra realigns with your newfound understanding, and you gain the next level in the Rasengan Class Mod.

### Rasengan

At 1st level in this Class Mod, you unlock the foundation of the Rasengan.

The Rasengan is a Technique created through the precise manipulation of chakra rotation, compression, and shape. Unlike most Techniques, the Rasengan does not require Hand Seals unless otherwise specified.

The Rasengan serves as the foundation for all **Rasengan Arts** you learn through this Class Mod.

You may form the Rasengan whenever you would normally be able to use a Technique, provided you have a free hand.

<br>

### Planetary Chakra

At 1st level in this Class Mod, you begin to understand the connection between your own chakra and the chakra that exists throughout the world around you.

You gain access to **Planetary Chakra**, a special resource used exclusively by certain Rasengan Arts.

Your Planetary Chakra Points are determined by the Rasengan Class Mod table.

Planetary Chakra Points are restored when you finish a Full Rest.

Planetary Chakra is separate from your normal Chakra pool and cannot be spent as normal Chakra.

\column
<br><br>
### Rasengan Arts

At 1st level of this Class Mod, you unlock the first techniques developed from your mastery of the Rasengan.

You gain access to ***Rasengan Arts***, a set of exclusive Techniques unique to this Class Mod. These are not standard Jutsu and do not require Hand Seals unless specified.

Rasengan Arts use **Planetary Chakra Points** as their primary resource when a cost is specified, and you gain additional Arts as you grow in this Class Mod. 

___

Whenever you gain a level in this Class Mod from 1st through 4th level, you gain 15 Rasengan Points, which may be spent to learn Rasengan Arts.

You select your Arts from the listed Rasengan Arts at the end of this Class Mod section.

These Arts use your **Rasengan Art Attack Bonus** or **Rasengan Art Save DC**.

___

To calculate your Rasengan Art Save DC and Attack Bonus, use the following formulas:

___

**Rasengan Art Save DC:** 10 + your Intelligence Modifier + 1/4 your Character Level;

**Rasengan Art Attack Bonus:** Your Intelligence Modifier + your Character Level + your Rasengan Class Mod Level

### Advanced Chakra Control

At 2nd level in this Class Mod, your control over chakra has reached the point where you can freely manipulate your Rasengan Arts without causing their structure to collapse.

You gain the following benefits:

- Whenever you use a **Rasengan Art** that deals Chakra damage, you may choose to deal its damage as **Chakra, Bludgeoning, or Force damage**.
- You may form a Rasengan Art without immediately releasing it. A Rasengan Art formed in this way remains maintained in your hand until you release it, dismiss it, or otherwise lose control of the Technique.
- You have advantage on any Ability Check made specifically to maintain control of a Rasengan Art.

Additionally, when you use a Rasengan Art, you may reduce its Planetary Chakra cost by **5**.

You may use this benefit a number of times equal to your Intelligence Modifier, regaining all expended uses when you finish a Long Rest.

### Rasengan Evolution

At 2nd level, your understanding of shape manipulation allows you to alter the structure of a Rasengan even after it has already been formed.

While maintaining a Rasengan Art that has not yet been released, you may use a **Bonus Action** to reshape it into another Rasengan Art you know.

If the new Rasengan Art has a higher Planetary Chakra cost than the Art currently being maintained, you must expend Planetary Chakra equal to the difference between the two costs.

If the new Rasengan Art has a lower Planetary Chakra cost, the difference is not refunded.

Reshaping a Rasengan Art does not release or activate it. The newly formed Art remains maintained and may be released normally.


---

### Spiral Echo

At 3rd level in this Class Mod, you have learned that the rotation created by a Rasengan does not need to end when the Technique itself is released.

When you hit a creature with a **Rasengan Art**, you may choose to imprint the rotation of that Art onto the target instead of immediately dealing its damage.

When you do so, record the damage dice that the Rasengan Art would have dealt. The target takes no damage from those dice at that time, but gains a **Spiral Echo** containing them.

Any other effects of the Rasengan Art resolve normally unless they specifically require the Art to deal damage.

A creature may have any number of Spiral Echoes placed upon it, and there is no maximum amount of damage that may be stored in this way.

Spiral Echoes do not disappear when you finish a Rest, move away from the affected creature, or after a certain amount of time has passed. The rotation remains within the target until you choose to release it.

As a **Bonus Action**, you may release any number of Spiral Echoes currently stored within one creature you are aware of.

When released, roll all stored damage dice contained within the chosen Spiral Echoes. The creature immediately takes the resulting damage as **Chakra damage**.

Alternatively, when a creature carrying one or more of your Spiral Echoes makes an Attack or activates a Technique, you may use your **Reaction** to release any number of Spiral Echoes stored within that creature before the triggering action resolves.

If you release at least **3 Spiral Echoes** in this way, the target must succeed on a Constitution Saving Throw against your Rasengan Art Save DC. On a failed save, the sudden release of rotational force disrupts the creature and interrupts the triggering Attack or Technique.

Once a Spiral Echo has been released, its stored damage is removed from the target.

Only you may willingly release Spiral Echoes created by your Rasengan Arts.

### Compressed Core

At 3rd level, you have learned to continue compressing a Rasengan after its initial formation, forcing increasingly greater amounts of rotational force into its core.

Whenever you are maintaining a **Rasengan Art** that has not yet been released, it gains one **Compression Level** at the end of each of your turns in which you continue to maintain it.

A Rasengan Art can hold a maximum of **3 Compression Levels**.

For each Compression Level stored within the Art:

- The Rasengan Art deals **one additional damage die** of the same type and size as its primary damage dice. This increases by one, each round.
- You gain a **+1 bonus** to its Rasengan Art Attack Roll. If the Art instead requires a Saving Throw, increase its Rasengan Art Save DC by **1**.

All Compression Levels are lost immediately after the Rasengan Art is released, dismissed, or you otherwise lose control of it.

If you use **Rasengan Evolution** to reshape the maintained Rasengan Art into another Rasengan Art, all accumulated Compression Levels are retained.

The additional damage dice granted by Compressed Core change to match the primary damage dice of the newly formed Rasengan Art.

### Perfect Rotation

At 4th level, you have achieved an extraordinary level of control over the rotation of your Rasengan Arts.

The rotation of your chakra remains perfectly stable even while you move, fight, or manipulate other forms of chakra.

You gain the following benefits:

- You no longer have disadvantage on Ranged Rasengan Art Attacks caused by being within 5 feet of a hostile creature.
- Whenever a Rasengan Art requires an Attack Roll, you may reroll an Attack Roll that results in a natural 1. You must use the new result.
- Creatures have disadvantage on Saving Throws made against your Rasengan Arts.
---
\column
### Mastered Rasengan

At 4th level, the Rasengan has become an extension of your chakra control rather than simply a Technique you perform.

Whenever you use a Rasengan Art, you may alter its rotation and structure immediately before the Technique resolves.

If the Art deals damage, you may reroll a number of its damage dice equal to your Intelligence Modifier. You must use the new results.

If the Art pushes or pulls a creature, you may increase that distance by up to **10 feet**.

If the Art affects an area, you may reduce the size of that area by any amount up to **5 feet**, allowing you to more precisely control which creatures are caught within the Technique.

Unlike ordinary Jutsu, Rasengan Arts are built upon the same fundamental Technique. By changing the rotation, compression, shape, or density of the chakra, the user can create entirely different applications of the Rasengan.

Unless an Art states otherwise, a Rasengan Art does not require Hand Seals.

### Rasengan: Uzuhiko
<br>

At 5th level in this Class Mod, your understanding of Planetary Chakra reaches its highest form.

Rather than immediately releasing the Planetary Chakra you gather, you have learned to bind it within the rotation of the **Rasengan: Uzuhiko**, allowing its power to accumulate over time.

You may transfer any amount of your available **Planetary Chakra Points** into the Rasengan: Uzuhiko. Planetary Chakra stored within the Uzuhiko is kept separately from your normal Planetary Chakra pool and remains stored until it is expended through an Uzuhiko Art.

This transfer is **permanent**.

Once Planetary Chakra has been stored within the Rasengan: Uzuhiko, it cannot be converted back into normal Planetary Chakra or used to activate ordinary Rasengan Arts.

There is no maximum amount of Planetary Chakra that may be stored within the Rasengan: Uzuhiko.

The amount of Planetary Chakra stored within the Uzuhiko represents the power you have accumulated from the world around you.


You gain access to the following **Uzuhiko Arts**:

- **Uzuhiko Assault**
- **Uzuhiko Reversal**
- **Uzuhiko Sanctuary**

Unlike ordinary Rasengan Arts, Uzuhiko Arts do not have a single fixed Planetary Chakra cost.

When activating an Uzuhiko Art, you choose how much Planetary Chakra stored within your Rasengan: Uzuhiko you wish to expend. The strength, area, duration, and additional effects of the Art are determined by the amount of stored Planetary Chakra released through it.

Planetary Chakra spent in this way is removed from the Rasengan: Uzuhiko.

### Chakra Conversion

The Rasengan: Uzuhiko is capable of permanently storing Planetary Chakra within its rotation.

During a **Long Rest or Full Rest**, you may transfer any amount of your currently available **Planetary Chakra Points** into the Rasengan: Uzuhiko.

There is no maximum amount of Planetary Chakra that can be stored within the Uzuhiko.

Planetary Chakra cannot be transferred into the Rasengan: Uzuhiko during combat, as the process requires prolonged concentration and precise control over the rotation of the stored chakra.

Once Planetary Chakra has been transferred into the Rasengan: Uzuhiko, the process cannot be reversed. Stored Planetary Chakra cannot be returned to your normal Planetary Chakra pool or used to activate ordinary Rasengan Arts.

Planetary Chakra stored within the Rasengan: Uzuhiko remains there until it is expended through one of the following Uzuhiko Arts:

- **Uzuhiko Assault**
- **Uzuhiko Reversal**
- **Uzuhiko Sanctuary**

Stored Planetary Chakra is not lost when you finish a Long Rest or Full Rest.
